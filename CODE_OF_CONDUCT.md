# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, argue with measurements rather than with people, and
credit the person who found the defect. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others'
private information are not tolerated.

To report a concern, write to the maintainer through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Reports are handled privately and within seven days.
